Google Maps tips

In order of impact

1

Add 50+ photos, then a few every week High

Loomey's has 13 photos. Main Beach Toys has 69, Little Freebirds 127, Engine Ear Toys 365. Add the storefront, the inside of the shop, gift wrapping, shelves by category (beach, pool, LEGO, baby) and staff.

2

Ask every happy customer for a review High

22 reviews is fewer than most competitors. Use a QR code at the register and a link on gift receipts. Reviews that mention things like "beach toys" or "baby gifts" help those searches.

3

Add categories and products for the gaps High

Not ranking for beach toys and barely ranking for LEGO. Add secondary categories (Baby store, Gift shop, Educational supply store) and Products on the profile for beach toys, pool toys, LEGO and baby gifts.

4

Post weekly on the profile Medium

New arrivals, holiday gift guides, events and the Palisades reopening. Regular posts tell Google the shop is active and give searchers a reason to click.

5

Fill in every attribute and Q&A Medium

Gift wrapping, curbside pickup, personal shopping and women-owned. Add your own Q&A answers, like "Do you gift wrap?" and "Do you carry LEGO?"

6

Set up the Palisades Village profile Medium

Claim and verify a separate profile for the reopened Palisades store, with its own photos, hours and reviews. It will be a different market with different competitors.

AI search tips

Get named in more answers

1

Create a "Curated Gift Boxes" page High

No engine named Loomey's for custom gift boxes in Laguna Beach (0 of 5). A page that explains the service, price range, how to order and delivery area gives AI something to cite.

2

Give each store its own page High

The Laguna Beach and Palisades Village stores share one Locations page. Separate pages with the address, hours, brands carried and neighborhood details help AI answer "near Forest Ave" and "in Palisades Village" questions.

3

Get listed where AI looks Medium

AI cited palisadesvillageca.com 17 times, plus Yelp, MapQuest, Yahoo Local, Pali Post and MomsLA. Keep those listings complete and pitch local parent blogs and gift guides.

4

Name brands and an FAQ on the site Medium

Google AI was the only engine to name Loomey's for NeeDoh and Palm Pals. Listing popular brands in plain text, and an FAQ about gift wrapping, curbside pickup and ages, gives every engine more to match.

03 · Website

Website tips for loomeystoys.com

These came from a review of the homepage and Locations page. Most of them can be changed in Shopify's settings without a developer.

No meta description

Neither the homepage nor the Locations page has one, so Google writes its own snippet.

Fix: Shopify → Online Store → Preferences. For example: "Boutique toy store on Forest Ave in Laguna Beach. Thoughtfully curated toys and gifts, free gift wrapping and curbside pickup."

Homepage title repeats the name

The title reads Loomey's Toys – Loomey's Toys, with no mention of toys in Laguna Beach.

Fix: "Loomey's Toys | Toy Store in Laguna Beach & Pacific Palisades"

No local business schema

The site only marks itself as an Organization. Google and AI can't read the address, hours or phone from the code.

Fix: Add ToyStore schema for each location with the address, hours, phone, geo coordinates and a link to the Google profile.

Missing image alt text

9 of 17 images on the homepage have no description.

Fix: Describe each image in plain words, like "Gift-wrapped toys at Loomey's Toys in Laguna Beach".

Heading doesn't say what or where

The main heading is "Discover a World of Wonder", which doesn't say toy store or Laguna Beach.

Fix: Keep the tagline and add a line under it: "Your neighborhood toy store in Laguna Beach and the Palisades".

Social preview says only "Loomey's Toys"

Links shared by text or social media show a one-line description.

Fix: Setting the meta description fixes this too. Also set a social sharing image of the storefront.

Add pages for the searches you're missing

Beach toys, LEGO, pool toys and baby gifts don't have their own pages.

Fix: Create a collection page for each, with a short intro that mentions Laguna Beach, so Maps and AI have a page to match.
